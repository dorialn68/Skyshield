Concept & engineering phase

Long-endurance autonomy, built around the mission.

AI.onSuper is AirShield.AI’s development program for a runway-based unmanned aircraft system. The design combines an efficient airframe, open mission architecture and human-authorized command for persistent ISR and special-mission payloads.

A note on status

AI.onSuper is a development program. Throughout this site, reference-aircraft data, current design assumptions and future performance objectives are identified separately.

Design approach

One air vehicle. A deliberate system architecture.

The platform is being developed as an integrated system: flight-critical functions remain separated from mission applications, payload interfaces stay modular and operator authority is defined from the outset.

01

Air vehicle

A long-span, runway-based aircraft with a configurable balance between fuel and mission payload.

02

Mission architecture

Open interfaces keep payload compute and mission applications separate from flight-critical control.

03

Command & control

Line-of-sight and beyond-line-of-sight communications are planned with redundant paths and defined lost-link behavior.

04

Human authority

Autonomous functions support navigation, sensing and decision support. Critical actions remain under designated operator authority.

Development program

An 18-month path from baseline to flight demonstration.

The program sequence moves from requirements and interface control through ground integration to a controlled flight-test campaign.

  1. 01M0–3

    Baseline & requirements

    Reference-aircraft acquisition, requirements freeze, safety planning and interface control.

  2. 02M3–6

    Preliminary design

    Structural and electrical adaptations, communications architecture and ground-control definition.

  3. 03M6–9

    Critical design & ground test

    Critical design review, mission-compute bench testing and payload integration.

  4. 04M9–12

    System integration

    Full ground runs and integrated demonstrations of sensors, control and data links.

  5. 05M12–18

    Flight validation

    First flight, envelope expansion and an integrated system demonstration.

Timing is a planning objective and depends on funding, supplier availability, permits, airworthiness activity and test approvals.
